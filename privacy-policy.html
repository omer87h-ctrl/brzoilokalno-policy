Privacy Policy

App: Brzo i Lokalno
Effective date: July 18, 2026
Contact: omer.creating.apps87@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Brzo i Lokalno collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes personal data in connection with the Brzo i Lokalno mobile application.

1. Who we are

Brzo i Lokalno is a local marketplace application that helps connect users, craftsmen, and creators through profiles, public works, job posts, offers, ratings, and communication features.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account data: email address, user ID, login-related information, and platform role selection (including Google Sign-In when used).
  • Profile data: display name, city, role, category, occupation, status, description, contact preferences, and other information you choose to add.
  • Representation and business profile data (when you choose to provide them): how you present yourself on the platform (as an individual or on behalf of a registered business entity), type of business entity, full registered business name, municipality/city of the business seat, business declaration version, and the time the business declaration was accepted. The app does not collect national ID numbers (JMBG), copies of ID documents, tax/business registration numbers (JIB), business registration decisions, or banking details for this feature.
  • User-generated content: works, photos, descriptions, job posts, offers, applications, ratings, and related content you create in the app.
  • Communication data: messages and related chat metadata when communication features are used.
  • Moderation and safety data: reports, blocks, abuse-prevention data, and records related to account safety and community protection.
  • Usage and technical data: technical, device, analytics, performance, crash, or diagnostic information processed through services used to operate, secure, and improve the app (for example Firebase/Google services such as Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, and App Check when enabled).
  • Weather query data: when weather planning features are used, the selected city name is sent to WeatherAPI to fetch forecast data for display in-app.

3. Purpose, necessity, and visibility of key profile categories

Platform role: used to route app features (seeking services vs offering services). It is required to complete registration. Without it, onboarding is not finished. Role-related information may be visible on profiles. Processed via Firebase/Google services and may be processed outside Bosnia and Herzegovina. Kept while the account exists. Access, correction, and deletion are available through profile editing, account deletion, or by contacting us.

Representation and business details (type, name, seat): used so other users can understand how you present yourself. Required for new registrations; older accounts without these fields can keep using the app, and the public profile may show that representation is not stated. On the public profile, representation type, business type, business name, and municipality/city of seat may be visible. These details are self-declared and are not treated as officially verified. Processed via Firebase services and may be processed outside Bosnia and Herzegovina. Kept while the account exists or until you change or delete them. Correction is available in Edit profile; deletion via account deletion or contact.

Business declaration version and acceptance time: used as a record that you confirmed your right to represent the stated business. Required only when you choose business representation. Not shown publicly. Kept with the account. Access/deletion via request or account deletion.

4. How we use data

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To provide profiles, public works, jobs, offers, applications, ratings, and messaging features.
  • To personalize and display content based on role, profile, and city.
  • To protect the app, detect abuse, investigate reports, and moderate harmful behavior.
  • To maintain app functionality, reliability, analytics, security, and service improvement.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies.

5. When data may be visible to others

Certain information may be visible to other users when you choose to publish it or use app features that display content publicly or to other users. This may include profile details, public works, ratings, job posts, offers, applications, and messages where applicable.

On a public profile, the following may also be visible when provided: how you present yourself, type of business entity, business name, and municipality/city of the business seat. A disclaimer may appear stating that the user provided the data and that Brzo i Lokalno has not verified the registration.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data to advertisers. We use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate the app, including Firebase / Google services (authentication, database, storage, push notifications, App Check, and related security features) and WeatherAPI (forecast data used for weather planning views). This means certain data are sent to those providers and may be processed outside Bosnia and Herzegovina.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to enforce our terms, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to protect users, the app, or the public.

7. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain account functionality, support legitimate business and safety purposes, and comply with legal obligations.

When a user requests account deletion, we attempt to delete or remove associated account data such as profile data, works, uploaded images, jobs, offers, applications, block records, and ordinary messages connected to the account.

In some cases, limited information may be retained or transformed for legitimate reasons such as security, fraud prevention, abuse handling, legal compliance, or record integrity. For example, some rating-related records may be anonymized instead of remaining linked to the deleted user account.

8. Account deletion

Users can request deletion of their app account and associated data through the in-app path and through the public deletion page: Delete Account.

In-app path: Settings > Delete Account

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy
